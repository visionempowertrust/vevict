Computational Thinking [CT] for persons with Visual Impairment [VI]

Victory for Inclusive Education

Project VICT turns the challenge of inaccessibility in Computational Thinking on its head.

Learning can be joyful and inclusive using games. Using the Ludic Design for Accessibility approach, VE introduces children with visual impairment to the joy of CT.

Skills Maintain skill categories, skill names, codes, sub-skills, and level indicators. Games Maintain games, play plans, and application levels linked to skills. Student session entry Record game sessions, application-level status, comments, and confidence score. Student dashboard Review latest status for every student and inspect full session history.
