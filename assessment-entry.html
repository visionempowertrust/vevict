Note to the Assessors

  1. Assessment should be conducted duly following the guidelines.
  2. During the assessment, no teaching should be involved.
  3. Typical time taken for the assessment per child should be around 20 mins.
  4. The mark should be given based on the following rubric: 0 for wrong answers; 0.25 for a question with 4 parts and 1 part answered properly; 0.5 for a partial answer or 2 parts correct in a 4 part question or 1 part correct in a 2 part question; 0.75 for 3 parts correct in a 4 part question; 1 for a completely correct answer.
  5. Students should be relaxed during the assessment.
  6. All tactiles needed should be prepared before the assessment.
  7. Sub-outcomes tested by each question should be reviewed while scoring. The overall CT outcome rating is calculated from marks: 0% to 30% is Missing, more than 30% to 75% is Adequate, and more than 75% is Acquired.
  8. Share your observations about the student in the free text in detail to know more about the student.
  9. If for any reason, you are not sure about scoring for the student, mark the Accuracy Score as Low.
